Privacy Policy

xlib's gallery & this website · Last updated October 17, 2026

This website is Aman Agrawal's personal portfolio. It includes "xlib's gallery" (Photo Drop), a private uploader that lets invited people share photos and videos with the site owner.

What Photo Drop collects

  • The photos and videos you choose to upload.
  • Your name, only if you type it in. It is saved as the description of your uploads.

Photo Drop doesn't ask you to sign in and never accesses your own Google account or files beyond the ones you pick.

How it is used and stored

Uploads are sent through a Google Apps Script run by the site owner and saved to a private album in the site owner's Google Photos account, where Google's privacy policy also applies. They are used only to keep and view the shared memories. They are not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone except people the owner chooses to share the album with.

Google API access

The app asks for permission only to add items to the owner's Google Photos library (the photoslibrary.appendonly scope). It cannot read, change, or delete existing photos. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Rest of the website

The portfolio uses Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics to count visits and understand which pages are used. Messages sent through the "Contact Me" app are delivered by email using EmailJS.

Removing your uploads

To have anything you uploaded removed, send a message through the "Contact Me" app on the website and it will be deleted.
